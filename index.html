How it works

01

Snap

Take one photo of your fridge. Just open the app and snap. You don't need to organize anything.

02

See

SousCheff reads what's in there. It picks up labels, expiration dates, and what's already starting to wilt.

03

Eat

You get a heads-up before things die. A small notification: "your strawberries have 2 days left." Eat them before you forget.

From the maker

I'm Gabriel. I'm 17, in DeSoto, Texas.

My family buys groceries every week and around $1,500 of it ends up in the trash every year. Not because we don't care. Nobody knows what's in the fridge until it's already gone bad. We just couldn't see what we had.

SousCheff fixes that. One photo of your fridge tells you what's there, what's about to spoil, and what to eat first. That's it.
