We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties. Your email address is used only by SousCheff for the purposes stated above.

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to better understand how you use our website. This helps us improve our site and user experience. Google Analytics collects basic information such as your browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

Unsubscribe

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it, and you'll be removed from our mailing list immediately. No questions asked.

Request Data Deletion

If you want us to delete your email address and any data associated with your account, please email us at souscheff.official@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Data." We'll remove your information within 5 business days and send you a confirmation.

Questions?
